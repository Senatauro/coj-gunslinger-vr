# Changelog

## 0.1.5

- QTE shootouts no longer ask for buttons: a dot marks the enemy, aim at it and shoot.
- Akimbo: you can reload one gun while shooting the other; akimbo reloads are 30% slower.
- Comfort vignette: Low, Medium and High now close in on the view while moving or turning, instead of only darkening
  the lens edge ([#3](https://github.com/Senatauro/coj-gunslinger-vr/issues/3)).
- Empty guns click again when you pull the trigger, without recoil or vibration
  ([#4](https://github.com/Senatauro/coj-gunslinger-vr/issues/4)).
- Leaning your head to dodge a bullet in Sense of Death no longer slides the body sideways
  ([#5](https://github.com/Senatauro/coj-gunslinger-vr/issues/5)).

## 0.1.4

- The wrist ammo counter no longer freezes after a long session
  ([#1](https://github.com/Senatauro/coj-gunslinger-vr/issues/1)).
